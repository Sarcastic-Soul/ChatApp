# Design direction

- **Who:** recruiters and developers opening a resume project on a laptop, plus
  friends trying the demo on a phone. They give it about 30 seconds.
- **Feel:** a well-kept paper notebook: warm paper, dark ink, one ink color for
  everything that matters. Calm, readable, not "techy".
- **Type:** Instrument Serif for display, Geist for body/UI. Big size gap.
- **Color:** one accent (deep green ink, user-switchable in the app), warm
  tinted neutrals, no pure black/white.
- **Must not look like:** a centered hero + 3x icon cards SaaS template,
  blue/purple gradients, glassmorphism, emoji icons, buzzword copy.
